# Instagram brands

Shared facts for every `ig-*` skill. Keep this file up to date; the skills
read it instead of asking the same questions again.

## TouriKo (@touri.ko)

- Line under the logo: "Holidays · Adventure". Tagline: "Every Trip, A Story".
- Colours: yellow #fbdd21, blue #004aad, orange #f28d28 (from the logo).
- Offers treks and trips. Bookings: by Instagram DM unless the user gives a
  phone or WhatsApp number.

## Trek & Travel with Sakhi (@_with_sakhi)

- Guided treks and study tours ("अभ्यासपूर्ण भटकंती") to Sahyadri forts and
  other places in Maharashtra, such as Raigad. Trips leave from Pune and Mumbai.
- Bookings happen over WhatsApp. `index.html` in this repository holds the
  current trip, date and price.

## Both brands

- Audience: Marathi-speaking trekkers and families, mainly Pune and Mumbai.
- Voice: warm, practical, proud of Maratha history and the forts.
- Language: Marathi first for anything the audience reads or hears, with
  simple spoken words rather than formal or translated-sounding ones. Reply
  to the user in the language they write in.
- Season: monsoon greenery (June–September), clear views and sunrise treks
  (November–February), heat and night treks in summer.

## Rules for all ig-* skills

- If the user does not say which brand, use the one the conversation is
  already about; if that is unclear, ask once.
- Never invent trip dates, prices, phone numbers, reviews, follower counts
  or results. Ask for missing facts, or leave a clearly marked blank.
- Do not promise virality or growth numbers.
- Do not copy another creator's post or video closely.
- Claude cannot open Instagram. Work from what the user pastes, uploads or
  describes, and say so when something could not be checked.
