# Instagram account audit

Read `.claude/ig-brands.md` first for the brands, voice, language and rules. If it is missing, ask the user for the account, niche, audience and language.

Claude cannot open Instagram or see private Insights. Ask the user for
what is needed and audit only what they provide.

## Ask for

- A screenshot of the profile (bio, Highlights, first 9–12 posts).
- Insights for the last 30 days if available: reach, followers gained,
  top posts, and for Reels the average watch time and shares.
- 3 posts that did well and 3 that did not.
- The goal: reach, followers, or bookings.

## Check

1. Profile: is it clear in 3 seconds who it is for and how to book?
2. Grid: consistent look, readable covers, recent activity.
3. Content mix: reach, trust and selling posts in balance.
4. Hooks: do the first 2 seconds or first line give a reason to stay?
5. Calls to action: is there one, and does it match the goal?
6. Pattern: how often and when they post, and gaps.
7. What the best posts have in common that the weak ones lack.

## Output

- A short verdict in 2–3 sentences.
- Top 5 fixes in priority order, each with what to change and how.
- What is working and should continue.
- What could not be judged because the data was not provided.

Base every statement on what the user shared. Do not guess numbers or
blame "the algorithm" without evidence.
