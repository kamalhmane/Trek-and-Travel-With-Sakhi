# Instagram Story sequence

Read `.claude/ig-brands.md` first for the brands, voice, language and rules. If it is missing, ask the user for the account, niche, audience and language.

## Steps

1. Take the purpose: promote a trip, drive people to a new post, show
   behind the scenes, collect questions, or share reviews.
2. Plan 4–7 frames. Each frame has one idea and one action at most.
3. Use an interactive sticker in at least two frames: poll, quiz,
   question box, emoji slider, countdown or link.
4. Put the main ask (DM, link, booking) in the last two frames, not the
   first.

## Output

For each frame: number, what is shown (photo, video or plain background),
text on screen (12 words at most), sticker and its exact wording, and
a reminder to keep text clear of the top and bottom edges.

Then suggest which frames to save into a Highlight and under what name.
