# Instagram Reel script

Read `.claude/ig-brands.md` first for the brands, voice, language and rules. If it is missing, ask the user for the account, niche, audience and language.

Turn one topic into a Reel one person can shoot on a phone. For a list of
ideas rather than one full script, use `ig-viral`.

## Steps

1. Take the topic, goal (reach, saves, bookings) and length from the
   request. Default: 20–35 seconds, goal reach.
2. Write the script as a table with one row per shot:
   time range, what is filmed, on-screen text, spoken line.
3. The first row is the hook: it must work in 2 seconds with the sound off.
4. Keep spoken lines short enough to say in the time given (about 2–3
   words per second in Marathi).
5. End on one call to action that fits the goal.

## Output

- Title, length and goal.
- The shot table.
- Full voiceover as one paragraph, ready to read aloud.
- Audio: voiceover, original sound, or "trending audio in your niche".
  Never name a specific trending sound without checking it.
- Caption (3 lines at most) and 3–5 niche hashtags.
- Cover text: 3–5 words for the Reel cover.
- What to film in advance (clips or photos needed).
